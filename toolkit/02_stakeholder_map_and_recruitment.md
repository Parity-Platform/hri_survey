# Stakeholder map and recruitment plan

Template. Replace text in angle brackets with local details.

## 1. Stakeholder map

| Group | Who | Why they matter | Channel to reach them | Target share of participants |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Manual workers | Production line, logistics and quality staff, including temporary and agency staff | Share the floor with the robot; most exposed to task change | Shift briefings, notice boards, worker representative | 35 to 40% |
| Operators and technicians | Staff who programme, monitor or maintain the robot | Daily interaction; know failure and recovery in practice | Team leader, maintenance meeting | 15 to 20% |
| Supervisors and managers | Line supervisors, team leaders, production and HR managers | Shape worker trust; manage the transition | Management meeting | 15 to 20% |
| SME clients | Purchasers and quality contacts of the SME's customers | Expectations on quality, delivery and responsible production | Account manager | 10% |
| Local community | Residents, vocational students, local associations, training centres | Future workforce; local labour market effects | Training centre, municipality, social media | 10 to 15% |

Representation targets for the whole sample:

- Gender: at least 40% of each gender among participants, or the share in the workforce if lower, stated explicitly
- Age: at least 20% of participants under 30 and at least 20% over 50
- Language: materials available in every language spoken by at least 10% of the workforce
- Seniority: at least one participant per shift and per department affected

## 2. Recruitment plan

| Week | Action | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Brief supervisors with the supervisor briefing pack | Coordinator |
| 1 | Announce the consultation to all staff (template in 06_communication_guidelines.md) | Management with worker representative |
| 2 | Distribute invitation and information sheet; open sign-up | Coordinator |
| 2 to 3 | Fill the participant register; check representation targets weekly | Coordinator |
| 3 | Targeted invitations to under-represented groups | Facilitator |
| 4 | Confirm interview and workshop slots within paid working time | Coordinator |

Rules:

- Participation is voluntary and takes place during paid working time.
- Supervisors do not select participants and do not see who took part.
- No participant is contacted more than twice if they do not reply.

## 3. Invitation text (email or printed notice)

> **Subject: Your view on robots in our production: invitation to a short consultation**
>
> <Company name> is considering the use of collaborative robots in <area / process>. No decision has been taken yet. Before any decision, <Company name> and <facilitating organisation> want to hear from the people who would work with this technology or be affected by it.
>
> You can take part in one or more of the following:
> - a 10 minute online or paper questionnaire
> - a 30 to 45 minute confidential interview
> - a 2 to 3 hour workshop with colleagues
>
> Participation is voluntary, takes place during paid working time and is confidential. Your manager will not know what you said. Results will be published in anonymous form and shared with all staff by <date>.
>
> To take part, contact <name, phone, email> or use the sign-up sheet at <location> by <date>.

## 4. Short notice for SME clients and community participants

Clients, residents, students and local organisations are invited to share their views through a short questionnaire or a public workshop on <date> at <venue>.
