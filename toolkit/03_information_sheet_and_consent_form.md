# Participant information sheet and consent form

Template aligned with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679). Replace text in square brackets. Have the final version reviewed by the data protection officer of the controller.

## Part A: Information sheet

**Title of the study:** [Study title, for example "Views on collaborative robots at Company name"]

**Organisations responsible:** [Controller: legal name, address]; [Facilitating organisation]

**Contact:** [Name, role, email, phone]. Data protection officer: [email]

### Why this study takes place

[Company name] is considering the use of collaborative robots in [area]. Before deciding, the company wants to understand how the people affected see the possible benefits, risks and conditions. The study does not promote or reject robots.

### What taking part involves

Depending on your choice: a questionnaire of about 10 minutes, an interview of 30 to 45 minutes, and/or a workshop of 2 to 3 hours. Interviews and workshops are audio recorded only if you agree.

### Voluntary participation

Taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any time and withdraw your data until [date of anonymisation], without giving a reason and without any consequence for your employment.

### What happens to your information

- Your answers are stored on [system] with access limited to [named roles].
- Names, contact details and any detail that could identify you are removed before analysis.
- Recordings are deleted after transcription and anonymisation, at the latest by [date].
- Your manager and colleagues will not see your individual answers.
- Anonymous results will be published openly, for example in dashboards and a report under an open licence, so that other organisations can learn from them. Groups smaller than five people are never reported separately.

### Legal basis and your rights

The legal basis is your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). You have the right to access, correct, erase and restrict the processing of your personal data, and to lodge a complaint with the national data protection authority [name and website].

### How results are used

Results will be shared with all participants and staff by [date]. The company will explain how the input was used in its decision.
